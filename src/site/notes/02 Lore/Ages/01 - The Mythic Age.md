**An age of stagnation**. The Mythic Age is marked by its timeless expanse; a never-ending blink of an eye that existed before the Primordial God, [[02 Lore/Gods/Primordial Gods/Chronos\|Chronos]] ended the stagnant world. Little is known about the age, and those that were alive to experience a world without time, are the Elder, and some Anceint, [[02 Lore/Bestiary/Dragons\|Dragons]]. They ruled the land gifted to them by their mother, their creator, [[02 Lore/Gods/Primordial Gods/Ymir\|Ymir]], coexisting with the [[02 Lore/Bestiary/Elementals\|Elementals]] of [[02 Lore/Gods/Primordial Gods/Enlil\|Enlil]].
